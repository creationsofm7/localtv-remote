Use your phone like a trackpad

Move the pointer

Glide one finger anywhere on the trackpad. Short, relaxed strokes give you the best control.

Click and drag

Tap once to click. For drag-and-drop, turn on Hold, then move with one finger and tap Hold again to release.

Right-click and scroll

Tap with two fingers for a right-click. Slide two fingers together to scroll up or down.
